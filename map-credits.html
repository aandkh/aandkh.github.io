Weather, air and environmental data

Weather observations, forecasts and alerts: NOAA / National Weather Service. Radar: NOAA MRMS. Forecast radar: NOAA HRRR, distributed by the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University. Rain budgets use preliminary NWS daily climate reports and 1991–2020 normals archived by IEM. Andymap converts units, selects regional readings and calculates comparisons; these are not official warnings or forecasts issued by Andymap.

Observed/interpolated air-quality data: U.S. EPA AirNow and reporting agencies, subject to the AirNow Data Use Guidelines. These data are preliminary, not fully validated, and unsuitable for regulatory determinations. Local conditions can differ from the mapped area.

Modeled air-quality forecasts: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS), supplied by Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0. Contains modified Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information 2026. Neither the European Commission nor ECMWF is responsible for any use that may be made of this information. Andymap selects nearby model grid cells, matches forecast hours and calculates outdoor-time and ventilation comparisons. These comparisons are not measurements at an address.

River observations, baselines, hydrography, terrain and geology: U.S. Geological Survey and named state geological sources. River data are provisional. Cycling and driving elevation profiles use USGS 3DEP ground elevations and Andymap sampling/smoothing, not surveyed roadway grades. Park elevation ranges use sampled 3DEP ground elevations, not surveyed high and low points.

Satellite imagery and nighttime light references: NASA Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA EOSDIS, including MODIS and Black Marble. Andymap selects dates and computes a historical nighttime-light proxy; it is not a current sky-brightness measurement. Cloud forecasts are NWS data. Flood-zone mapping: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer, via Esri USA Flood Hazard Areas (March 3, 2026 snapshot). Regional boundaries are clipped and simplified by approximately 90 metres for an overview. Not current flooding or a property-specific determination.

Traffic and transport

Traffic flow © TomTom. All rights reserved. Full TomTom and supplier copyright notices. Traffic data remain subject to the applicable TomTom license; a map credit does not grant redistribution rights.

Missouri traffic incidents and work zones: Missouri Department of Transportation; official WZDx feed is CC0 1.0. Delays are displayed only when reported. Flight positions: adsb.lol, ODbL 1.0, with incomplete receiver coverage.

Metro Transit schedules and reported vehicle locations: Bi-State Development / Metro Transit, provided as-is under its data terms. Andymap is not an official Metro product and no endorsement is implied. Routes, service and actual operation may differ.

Joyride routing uses OSRM with OpenStreetMap road data. Cycling routes combine OpenStreetMap geometry with Great Rivers Greenway route descriptions and published access information. Andymap selects and calculates route comparisons; it does not infer current closures, traffic or safe travel speeds.

Local sources and derived data

Event and screening facts come from official venue, organizer, university, team and cinema calendars linked on individual entries, including the independent operators retained by Andymap. Factual titles, times, places and source links are selected and normalized; artwork and promotional descriptions are not republished. Schedules, availability and admission can change.

Market listings: USDA Local Food Directories and listing owners. Business and food-truck facts are credited to their operators on the corresponding entries. City/County census statistics and boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau. Source dates and measurement limits remain attached to displayed records.

OpenStreetMap-derived park, road, trail and place data, including park area, clipped paths, woodland, water, mapped facilities and road-distance comparisons, retain OpenStreetMap contributor credit and the ODbL 1.0. Andymap filters regional records, simplifies geometry and calculates original comparisons. Public source files and attribution metadata are available in the site repository; original data can be obtained from the source links. A regional extract is not a complete local directory.

Privacy and advertising setup

Saved maps, display preferences and short-lived data caches are stored in this browser. Using the location button requests browser permission; location is used to center the map and request nearby results. Map coordinates and requested areas are sent to the services needed to deliver those results. Third-party map, weather and data providers receive normal network information such as the IP address and request details under their own policies.

Google AdSense ownership verification is present as a metadata tag and an ads.txt publisher record. This version does not load the AdSense ad script, request ads, reserve ad spaces or enable Auto ads. AdSense approval is controlled by Google. Advertising and any necessary privacy/consent implementation must be reviewed before ad delivery is enabled.
