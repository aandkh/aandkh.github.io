# Credits and advertising setup

Andrew requests unobtrusive credits, while meeting actual provider terms.
`map-attribution.js` supplies readable linked OpenMapTiles/OpenStreetMap credits
and conditional TomTom copyright. The OSM fallback always retains its required
visible credit. The information control opens a small same-page source list;
`map-credits.html` contains full provider, adaptation, software and privacy notices.
Do not hide required credits behind panels, offscreen or in unreadable text.
The credit strip's measured height reserves room under the toolbar and sheets.
Check phone, desktop, shared links, map styles, Traffic on/off and open panels.

Provider notices in the relevant displays also matter: AirNow requires source
agency credit and preliminary status; CAMS/Open-Meteo credit appears beside the
modeled forecast, with license/modification/disclaimer details nested; MassOutage
credits retain original observation times, source links and upstream names when
supplied. Do not substitute fetch time for observation time or fabricate an
upstream supplier. Use the original regional feed's sources/metadata where available.
Metro terms do not grant trademark/logo permission; text source credit suffices.

Keep all supplied vendor and style licenses. SunCalc 1.9.0 is BSD 2-Clause,
MapLibre GL JS BSD 3-Clause, its Leaflet adapter ISC and Instrument Sans OFL1.1.
OpenStreetMap-derived datasets retain ODbL notices/source access. Longer notices
may be kept on the linked credits page. Credits do not create redistribution
rights or replace account-specific provider agreements.

AdSense is restored for ownership verification only:
`<meta name="google-adsense-account" content="ca-pub-8173323521375339">`
and root `ads.txt`. No AdSense JS, ad requests, Auto ads or placeholders are loaded.
This uses Google's documented meta verification method and does not claim Google
approval or submit an account review. Before enabling actual ads, Andrew must
authorize that separate change and the privacy/consent implementation must match it.
