# Regional weather readings

The default camera and the four north, south, east and west weather sectors are fixed. Weather cards use the reporting station's coordinates, temperature, sustained wind and original measurement time. The station can change. Selection and data-source details are in [Metro observation cards](METRO-WEATHER-MAINTENANCE.md).

The landfill model uses all eligible wind reports in the region. The four visible cards are a sample for the map overview. [Landfill estimates](LOCAL-AIR-MEASUREMENTS.md#landfill-estimates-in-the-air-quality-fill) documents how winds affect the shading.
