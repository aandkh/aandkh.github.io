What Stronghold is

A kingdom, and one move a day to run it with.

You rule one province. It is not the important one. The crown of your own realm sits on somebody else's head, and he has his own ambitions, his own opinion of you, and no particular interest in your advancement.

The game is what you do about that.

Nobody hands you the answer

There is no calculator here, and no screen that tells you whether something will work. What you have instead is people. Your marshal has a view. Your spymaster is confident, or he is guessing, and he will say which.

Counts a clerk would read from a ledger stay numbers. Judgements a person would form come back as words.

Three wagons of them outside Kelm, half without boots.

So you decide the way the job actually works: from what the people around you say, knowing some of them are wrong.

The world writes back

Your court is a set of people, named the day you begin. Soldiers write home. Merchants complain about tolls. Villages petition about the levy. Rival lords remember exactly how you have treated them, and say so.

None of it is decoration, and none of it prints a statistic at you.

Where it runs

In a browser, on a phone or a desktop. Your kingdom is wherever you sign in. It is free, nothing in it can be bought, and no payment improves your position in any way.
