How a turn works

Read what happened. Give the orders. Submit.

A turn is not a session you play through. It is a morning at a desk.

You arrive to a day already in progress and read what came in overnight. Then you move around your administration setting whatever the day calls for. None of it happens while you are deciding; the day resolves when you submit it, and not before.

Nothing is lost while you think. What you have set is kept with your kingdom rather than in the page, so you can start on a phone, close it, and pick it up somewhere else. It will be where you left it.

Then you submit, and it is done. Orders cannot be recalled.

One move a day

That is the whole shape of it. You get today's move today.

Signing in

Pick a username and a password you use nowhere else. They exist for one reason, which is to keep your kingdom apart from everybody else's. There is nothing here worth stealing: no email, no payment details, nothing about you.

Ending a campaign deletes it, and everything attached to it, at once and for good.
