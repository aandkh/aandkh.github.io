# Metro observation cards

The reference points are Arnold, Lambert, Spirit of St. Louis Airport and Missouri Botanical Garden. In each geographic sector, choose the newest usable observation with temperature and wind within 12 km. A nearby reporter can replace a working primary. When an area lacks a full report or the choices overlap, a station within 20 km can cover the quiet area. Such a station can lie up to 4 km beyond the nearest-sector boundary. Every pair retains at least 60% of its original separation and at least 40% of the original separation projected along the line between the reference points. Among equally complete layouts, prefer the four local sectors, then minimize the age of the oldest reading. The default map camera stays fixed. Leader dots and forecast requests follow the actual instrument.

Cards expire at 20 minutes from observation. Partial reports can supply individual fields during outages. An empty sector displays dashes. The map checks every minute while visible and on return to the page. Official forecasts and their valid times still belong to forecast playback.

## Observations

The five-minute regional weather workflow refreshes one small file on the dedicated weather-observations-live branch. It reads a regional extract from [WxAlerts](https://wxalerts.org/docs/api/observations/) and the University of Missouri stations at the [Science Center](https://agebb.missouri.edu/weather/realtime/stlouissciencecenter.asp) and [Moscow Mills](https://agebb.missouri.edu/weather/realtime/moscowmills.asp). The map credits WxAlerts for its processing. The file retains individual measurement times and excludes withheld fields. The university clock explicitly gives CST or CDT. When publication has been delayed by at least eight minutes, the collector takes three additional samples four minutes apart. Every publication requires a valid source response.

The collector also reads a single bounded regional weather table from [findU](http://www.findu.com/cgi-bin/wxnear.cgi?lat=38.67&lon=-90.41&limit=85&noold=1). Only registered CWOP weather stations with matching catalog coordinates, distance and compass direction qualify. Report times use the table’s original server date and packet age, rounded down to the minute. Ambiguous rain totals cannot establish dry conditions.

The browser independently checks [Iowa Environmental Mesonet airport observations](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/1/currents.json?networkclass=ASOS&wfo=LSX), the Botanical Garden and [NWS station histories](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api). Bounded NWS histories also fill neighborhood gaps. The radar cards and air layer share in-flight requests and their short caches. NWS QC failures, future dates and impossible units or values are omitted. The regional feed uses the same fixed geographic bounds for every visitor.
