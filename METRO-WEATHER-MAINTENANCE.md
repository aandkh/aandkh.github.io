# Metro observation cards

Arnold, Lambert, Spirit of St. Louis Airport and Missouri Botanical Garden anchor the four sectors. The cards search real stations up to 26 km from those reference points inside the fixed overview. Candidate stations can cross a sector boundary by up to 4 km. The layout retains at least 60% of each pair’s original distance and 40% of its projected separation along the original axis. Leader dots, place labels and forecast links follow the reporting instrument.

The selector first fills the four sectors with temperature and sustained wind. It minimizes the age of the oldest report, then the combined age, then distance to the original locations. A closer station wins when times tie. Reports under eight minutes are preferred; recent measured backups may fill gaps up to 25 minutes, with their real observation times shown. Older observations expire. The map checks every 30 seconds while visible and rechecks on wake or reconnection. If every reachable station in a sector stops reporting, the card shows the fields available. The timestamp always belongs to the instrument.

## Station feeds

The regional collector takes a sample every two minutes. Its public-repository GitHub Actions job publishes four samples, queues a successor before the first sample and keeps collecting during the handoff. Each sample and external command has a deadline, and the snapshot is replaced atomically. Concurrency permits one active publisher. A separate five-minute schedule can restart collection if the handoff fails. Each publication updates only the single-file weather-observations-live branch. The map compares the raw publication with the repository contents endpoint using the stations’ own observation times. Visitors share the same URLs in each update window. A public CDN supplies another copy if both routes are delayed.

The collector combines [WxAlerts](https://wxalerts.org/docs/api/observations/), [NOAA MADIS surface observations](https://madis-data.ncep.noaa.gov/public/sfcdump.shtml), fixed CWOP/APRS reports via [findU](http://www.findu.com/cgi-bin/wxnear.cgi?lat=38.67&lon=-90.41&limit=150&noold=1), and the [University of Missouri Mesonet](https://agebb.missouri.edu/weather/realtime/stlouissciencecenter.asp). It matches findU’s weather table to coordinates in its weather-flagged position table or the verified station catalog. Only fixed, individually verified amateur calls and registered CWOP weather calls qualify. The table’s distance is approximately 60 times the Euclidean distance between coordinates in degrees. Geographic card spacing and bearings use local distances in kilometers. The weather table’s own response date and packet age supply the report time, conservatively rounded down to the minute.

Browsers also read [Iowa Environmental Mesonet airport observations](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/1/currents.json?networkclass=ASOS&wfo=LSX), Botanical Garden and [National Weather Service station histories](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api). The card publisher and the air layer retain their separate time limits. No model output enters these station readings.

Run the metro-weather, weather-loading, weather-integration, air-wind-screening and weather-ingestion tests. To verify production, check that the observation branch advances between collector samples and that all four displayed times and dots match the selected reports.
