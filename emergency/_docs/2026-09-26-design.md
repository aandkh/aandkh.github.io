# Chill (family emergency app mock): design

Status: approved by the owner in chat on 2026-09-26. Name: **Chill**. Look:
Tiles for Home, Atlas for emergency screens, both in a dark theme ("Bright is
not Right for this type of app"). This file is the spec for the mock and the
handoff for anyone picking it up later.

## What it is

A design mock the owner and a friend open on their phones at
`https://www.andrewhanes.com/emergency/`. Everything is simulated on the
device: a pretend family, a drawn map, pretend GPS, pretend notifications.
Two phones do not talk to each other. The real map API and a backend come
later (see "The real version" at the end).

Ethos: everyone is private and free until an emergency. Then location opens
up, and every person is told plainly who can see them and why.

## Access

- Sign-in gate, username `kai`, password `kai`. The code stores a SHA-256
  digest, not the words. It is a speed bump: the repo is public, so anyone
  can read the code. Remembered on the device after the first sign-in.
- `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`. Nothing links to the page.
- Plain-http visits are redirected to https (Pages does not enforce it here).

## The pretend family

Mom, Dad, Ava (16), Leo (13). After sign-in you pick who you are; the demo
panel switches later. Each person sets a 4-digit PIN the first time you play
them.

## Visibility rules (the core)

A viewer V can see member M's location when any of these hold:

1. M is V (you always see yourself).
2. M is sharing on purpose and the share has not expired.
3. An emergency pressed by M is active (the presser is visible to everyone).
4. An emergency pressed by V is active (the presser sees everyone).

Nothing else. During Ava's emergency Mom cannot see Leo unless Leo shares.
A share of kind "until this emergency ends" stops when that emergency ends.

Whenever anyone other than you can see you, a banner names them and the
reason. When that stops, you are told once that you are private again.
Every change of visibility goes in the shared log that the whole family can
read, so nobody can look quietly.

## Structure

Two tabs: **Home** (the button; settings behind a gear) and **Map** (always
available, always a map). Private Mode, when on, replaces the whole UI.

## Home (Tiles look)

Bento grid on the dark ground, floating dock with Home | Map.

- **SOS tile** (full width, red): press and hold to start an emergency.
- **Your location**: Private / Sharing until 9:40 PM / Sharing until you
  stop / Visible (emergency). Share opens 1 hour, 4 hours, Until I stop;
  during someone else's emergency also "Until this emergency ends".
- **Silent alert**: press and hold; same visibility, but the presser's phone
  stays quiet and calm-looking.
- **Sharing tiles**: one wide tile per person you can currently see, with a
  mini map. None: "Nobody is sharing. Everyone's private."
- **Check-in timer**: set "in 30 min / 1 hr / 2 hr" or a time, with an
  optional note that stays sealed unless the check-in is missed.
- **Nightly check-in**: a daily time with an on/off switch.
- **Family**: avatars and counts; opens a list of everyone's status
  (never a location you are not allowed to see).

## Press and hold (SOS and silent alert)

- Threshold 500 ms, the iOS Haptic Touch "Default" duration that iMessage's
  bubble menu uses (iOS 17+ "Fast" is about 200 ms; a website cannot read
  that setting, so we match Default).
- During the hold the tile presses in, the way an iMessage bubble does.
  Moving the finger more than 10 px or scrolling cancels.
- At the threshold: haptic, then the alert fires. Haptic is
  `navigator.vibrate` on Android; on iPhone it is the iOS 18 trick of
  clicking a label bound to a hidden `<input type="checkbox" switch>`.
  Whether iOS allows that from a timer is untested; it may not fire.
- An accidental press is cancelled with the PIN ("Pressed it by accident?").
  The same PIN is required for "I'm safe", so someone who grabs the phone
  cannot end a real alert.

## Emergency (Atlas look)

Full-screen map, red floating banner (time since start + who sees whom),
white bottom sheet with the Home | Map switch at its foot.

- **Presser**: "Help is on the way" once anyone is coming, else "Your
  family has been alerted". A row per person: Not seen yet, Seen, On the way
  (with ETA), plus distance. Call 911 (the mock shows a notice and never
  dials). I'm safe (PIN). Cancel an accident (PIN).
- **Everyone else**: a full-screen alert first (sound and vibration), then
  the map: the presser's pin, yours, and a sheet with I'm on my way, Call
  (notice only), Share my location with everyone (until the emergency ends),
  and Mark resolved (only after confirming you reached them; logged with
  your name). Silent alerts say the presser may not be able to talk: text,
  don't call. Missed check-ins show the unsealed note.
- **Map tab** during an emergency: the same map, full screen.

## Modes

- **Emergency button**: loud alert, full visibility both ways.
- **Silent alert**: same visibility; presser's screen shows only a small
  gray "Silent alert active" line with End (PIN).
- **Check-in timer**: reminder 5 minutes before; at the deadline a 2-minute
  last call on the owner's phone; missing it starts an emergency of kind
  "missed check-in" (same visibility rules).
- **Nightly check-in**: the same, daily. A check-in within the 3 hours
  before the deadline counts for that night.
- **Timed share**: 1 hour, 4 hours, until you stop, or until the current
  emergency ends.

## Settings

Profile and PIN, nightly check-in, Private Mode, Peace of Mind, the shared
log, demo controls, sign out.

## Peace of Mind

A button in settings, usable once every 30 seconds (the button shows the
countdown). It opens a side panel that pings each other person and shows two
responses per person, each with a friendly green check when it passes (the
owner's spec):

- **Phone: On** (the phone answered this check), else **Phone: No answer**.
- **GPS: Located** (the phone got a fresh GPS fix), else **GPS: Off**. Not
  checked when the phone did not answer.

If a phone answered but its alerts are switched off, a small note says so
under its two lines, since two green checks would otherwise imply it will hear
an emergency. The panel never shows a position.

Rule, and the owner's words: "NO LIES". Verified means that phone answered
this exact check, just now, with a fresh fix. No answer is Not verified.
Nothing cached counts. In the mock there are no phones, so the panel is
labeled "Demo: simulated results", and the demo panel can knock a phone
offline or turn its location or alerts off to show the failures.

## Private Mode (disguise)

Turned on in settings. The app then opens straight into a text
conversation that looks like Messages:

- **Look**: iMessage (iPhone) or Google Messages (Android). Auto picks by
  device; settings can force either. It uses the phone's own system font.
  Best when added to the Home Screen, so there is no browser address bar.
- **Conversation**: a preset (several, each with a scrollable history over
  a few days) or custom (contact name and messages).
- **Hold any bubble** (500 ms iMessage, 400 ms Android, with the haptic):
  the normal reaction menu appears as it would in the real app, and a
  silent alert goes out. One second later the contact starts "typing" for
  20 seconds, then stops. That is the confirmation.
- **Hold the message box**: opens settings.
- **Hold send** (or the microphone that sits there when the box is empty):
  opens the Map tab.
- **Typing and sending** works. iMessage shows "Delivered" under your last
  sent text; Android shows Sending, then Delivered. Typed texts delete
  themselves after 24 hours. They go nowhere; settings says so in one line,
  so nobody in trouble waits for a reply.
- Alerts from family arrive as an ordinary text from the contact. Sending
  any text while a check-in is due counts as checking in.
- Someone snooping who holds a bubble also sends the silent alert. The PIN
  cancels it.

## Demo panel

A small "Demo" tab (hidden in Private Mode; reach it through settings):
switch who you are; make someone else press the button, send a silent
alert, or miss a check-in; set each phone OK / offline / location off /
alerts off; skip time ahead; sound on or off; reset.

Pretend family behavior: when you press, Mom sees it after about 2 s, Dad
after 4 s and heads over at 6 s (his pin drives toward you), Leo sees it
after 20 s.

## Not in the mock

No backend, push, real GPS, accounts, or cross-device sync. Call 911 never
dials.

## The real version (told to the owner)

A website cannot read GPS in the background or buzz an iPhone reliably, so
background location during emergencies and a truthful Peace of Mind need
native iOS and Android apps, a server for the family group, and push. The
disguise cannot be guaranteed indistinguishable from the real Messages app.
